Before you start

Questions people ask us.

Is this an official Goethe-Institut certificate?

No — GermanExamHero is exam preparation, not a certificate. You still book and sit the official A1 exam at Goethe-Institut, telc, or ÖSD. We get you ready to pass it.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

It works best if you already know a little basic vocabulary. If you're starting from absolute zero, spend a couple of weeks with free beginner lessons first, then come back — the adaptive plan will meet you where you are.

How long until I'm ready for the exam?

Most learners feel exam-ready in 4–8 weeks of steady practice. The mock exams tell you honestly when your predicted score clears the 60% pass mark.

What if I fail the real exam?

You keep lifetime access, so you can keep practicing at no extra cost. One retake of the official exam usually costs more than this whole course.

Can I use it from my country?

Yes. Everything runs online and works from anywhere in the world, in any timezone. Grammar can be explained in your own language, too.

How do I pay?

Secure card and PayPal payments are supported. Regional pricing is available for eligible countries so the course stays affordable wherever you are.
